    What we bring

    The instruments that find what eyes can’t.

    Every defect in the walkthrough was found with one of these — nothing drilled, nothing broken. Calibrated instruments, read by an inspector who knows what the numbers mean.

    Thermal imaging camera

    Reads surface temperature rather than looks. Escaping water cools the material around it, so a leak sealed inside a wall shows up as a cold trail through plaster and tile.

    Hidden pipe leak behind the toilet

    Moisture meter

    Measures how much water is held inside the plaster, not just on the surface. Fresh paint hides a damp patch; the meter still reads the wet wall underneath.

    Damp patch & mould on the outer wall

    Socket & earth tester

    Checks live, neutral and earth at every outlet. A socket with no earth powers your appliances perfectly — right up until a fault makes the casing live.

    Scorched socket with no earthing

    Tile tapping rod

    A tile bedded on full adhesive rings solid; one laid over an air pocket answers with a dull hollow tock. Every floor tile gets tapped, and each hollow one is marked.

    Hollow & cracked floor tiles

    Crack width gauge

    Puts a number on a crack. Width is what separates harmless shrinkage from movement that needs a structural engineer — and it decides whether filler is honest or a cover-up.

    Crack running from the door corner

    Laser level & straight-edge

    Projects a true horizontal and vertical to compare against. Floors that fall the wrong way, walls out of plumb and tile lippage are all obvious against a dead-straight line.

    Water pools away from the drain

    Borescope camera

    A lens on a flexible probe that sees inside ducts, shafts and the cavity under a sink unit — the places a leak starts, without opening anything up.

    Leak under the kitchen sink

    Water spray & flood test

    Recreates a monsoon on demand. Water is sprayed at the outside of a frame, or ponded on a wet-area floor, to prove the seal and the fall before the rain does.

    Leaking window frame

    Clamp meter & RCD tester

    Measures the current a circuit really draws and times how fast the trip actually cuts power. An overloaded board runs hot long before it fails.

    Overheating distribution board
